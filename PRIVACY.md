# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-18

Kata does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network requests.

**What stays on your device.** Your study progress, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your device (in SwiftData and UserDefaults). They are removed when you delete the app.

**Reminders.** If you turn on daily reminders, they are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/meysampg/Kata/issues.
